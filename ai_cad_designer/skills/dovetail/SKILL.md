# Dovetail

- Use a 0.25 mm clearance on each sliding face for calibrated PETG printers.
- Prefer a shallow 10–15 degree flank over steep undercuts.
- Add a lead-in chamfer or enlarged first 2 mm of travel.
- Keep the rail parallel to the intended print layers and inspect elephant-foot risk.
- Use a separate stop or snap detent when the joint must resist axial motion.
