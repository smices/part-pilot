# Mortise and tenon

- Reserve 0.25 mm clearance per side for removable PETG joints.
- Use a tenon length of at least twice its thickness.
- Round or chamfer the entry; do not force a sharp rectangular corner into an FDM mortise.
- Add a snap detent only when pull-out retention is required.
- Keep the mortise wall at least 2.5 mm thick around the opening.
