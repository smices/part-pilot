# Screwless joint selection

- Snap fit: quick access panels and covers.
- Dovetail or sliding rail: guided drawers and structural modules.
- Mortise and tenon: keyed replaceable mounts.
- Magnet: frequent access when purchased magnets and adhesive are acceptable.
- Prefer fewer joint families per product and make disassembly direction obvious.
- Never call a joint serviceable unless the release path remains accessible.
