# Snap fit

- Default material: PETG.
- Default designed clearance: 0.25 mm per mating interface.
- Keep cantilever roots at least 2.0 mm thick and avoid sharp internal corners.
- Use four distributed hooks for enclosure retention instead of one highly loaded hook.
- Add relief slots beside the beam and keep hook projection near 1.0–1.2 mm.
- Orient the beam so layer lines run along its length when practical.
- Validate both the male hook and female window with OpenCascade before export.
